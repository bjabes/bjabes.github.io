Why This Book Matters

Gunpei Yokoi was the legendary Nintendo engineer behind the Ultra Hand, Game & Watch, and Game Boy. His philosophy of "lateral thinking with withered technology" shaped Nintendo's identity and changed the gaming industry forever. This 1997 book by Yokoi and journalist Takefumi Makino has never been officially translated into English—until now.
